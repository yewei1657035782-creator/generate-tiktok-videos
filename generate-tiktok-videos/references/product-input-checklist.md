If the product is already registered, provide only its product ID (for example `PV-0001`) plus the new campaign request. Re-submit images or parameters only when the registered product has changed.

## Minimum viable submission

At minimum, provide:

1. Product name/SKU and exact set contents.
2. Dimensions of the overall product and major components.
3. Material, color, and finish for each major component.
4. Confirmed structure or moving-part behavior.
5. Three prioritized selling points with evidence.
6. Intended environment and target user.
7. One clean hero image plus material and structure close-ups.

If this minimum is incomplete, proceed only with an appearance-led concept and label material, structure, dimension, function, and performance shots as provisional.
